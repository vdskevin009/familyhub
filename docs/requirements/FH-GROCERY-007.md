# FH-GROCERY-007 — Equal reference quantities for comparisons

Status: Testing. Work item: #205; follow-up to #203/#155. Explicitly authorized by Kevin on 2026-10-12. Supersedes only the FH-GROCERY-006 exclusion of unknown household physical amounts from display-only common totals.

Preserve each calculable known basket quantity. If the quantity/desired pack is unknown, a counted portion has no known weight, or uses have no sourced dose/capacity, select one common evidenced comparison dimension for that article and use 1 kg, 1 litre, 1 unit or one labelled use at every selected store. A count without weight may use a separately labelled weight reference; a use without a dose may use a physical-volume reference. This is never a conversion of the recorded need, density, slice weight or consumption. Known mass and volume are never converted or replaced across dimensions.

Every reference line is labelled as such, with the unit/quantity/conversion in Details. The summary counts reference lines and says comparison basket, different from the real basket. Actual whole-pack purchase cost remains separate from proportional comparison cost; neither is a household consumption estimate. Keep the existing identical-subset rule, source/format/mode/currency/expiry/availability filters and unknown prices. No price, format, stock, density or dose is invented. Explicitly chosen unpriced stores cannot silently disappear or yield a misleading lowest partial amount. Counts reflect truly shared sourced references, not a forced 14/14.

Frontend only. Preserve all saved household quantities, selections, preferences, approvals, sources and worker/private state. No worker/API/schema change, ordering, subscription, new service or automatic research/import. Regression cases cover unknown quantities/packs, portion counts, uses without dose, known quantities, explicit null basket overrides, incompatible dimensions, missing stores/prices, identical reference quantities and plan preservation. Browser checks cover expansion to 14 shared synthetic articles, store selection, detailed reference explanation, keyboard/reload/no writes and 320/390/768/1440 layouts. Publication and physical-phone acceptance remain separate.
