# FH-GROCERY-010 — Purchase-first Achats

Status: Testing. Issue #155. Code and protocol references: `grocery-purchase-evidence.ts`, `grocery-planning-store.ts`, `grocery-purchases.ts`, and `GroceryPurchases.tsx`. Release verification is separate from source implementation.

Optional plan `sourceEstimates` preserves reviewed estimated per-ordered-unit quotes separately from raw confirmation amounts. Each entry binds the exact source line, quantity, currency, unit price, provenance and review date; explicit confirmed ingestion and `grocery-source-estimates-v1` are required. The display computes quantity times unit quote once, rounds the line estimate to cents, and never multiplies again by physical package size. Zero-filled source total columns are not evidence of a free product. Current catalogue prices never replace source quotes. Unit prices and line amounts remain labeled estimated, independently of competitor coverage. The sum of known estimated rows shows coverage; a fully covered row sum differing from the source total is explicitly flagged for taxes/fees/adjustment reconciliation without changing that total. No paid amount or insurer/finance transaction is inferred. Existing evidence survives omission by legacy clients.

Reviewed equivalent-format comparisons are separate derived metadata in optional plan `purchaseComparisons`, never mutations of source receipt lines. Each proof binds a record, line index, exact raw-line key and exact current public-price key; it records whole-format quantity, source/competitor format labels, explicit comparison-only equivalence, dated review/provenance and an optional evidenced source estimate. It never claims SKU/brand identity or changes household substitution permissions, basket membership, payment classification or raw uncertainty. Unknown rows remain unknown. Source/price changes, incompatible formats, stale/future prices and ambiguous matches fail closed.

Only an explicitly confirmed save may add a proof after server-side validation against the actual saved source. Evidence is additive and retained if an older client omits it; conflicting replacement IDs and stale plan revisions fail. Worker `grocery-comparison-evidence-v1` is required for client writes. Valid local same-format public observations can support a product-only comparison even when purchase mode, home serviceability, fees or buying-substitution approval remain unknown: those limitations remain visible, and the proof is never consumed by shopping-cart recommendations. Partial coverage shows a current-observation subtotal against an estimate for exactly the same source rows, with an observed difference rather than a guaranteed order saving. No complete total is extrapolated.

The approved purchase cards contain PCX, date and amount, then simple Walmart/Costco rows in the same box. Do not show a delivered/payment-status line on the card. « Total estimé » identifies an original confirmation; delivery alone never certifies final payment. Default view contains source-backed completed PC Express purchases in the last 30 Vancouver calendar days (today included). Duplicates are excluded; other, older, unfulfilled and draft tickets remain accessible in collapsed Archives without deletion. Import and monthly history remain under Sources et outils. Detail restores compact per-product store/real-format/price rows; source conditions, exclusion reasons, calculations and separate whole-pack outlay are disclosures, not repeated paragraphs on the entry card.

Optional `fulfillment: {date,evidence}` preserves the dated same-order actual-delivery source separately from the original confirmation date and total. Missing/null means unknown, not delivered. A scheduled slot, picked/in-transit notice or current PC Express cart is not completion evidence. It requires a valid date and nonempty source citation; extraction is conservative, review retains the field and writes require `grocery-fulfillment-v1`. Older receipts remain valid. Source 2.26.11 advertises support; the installed worker remains unchanged until separately authorized release. Conflicting substitution evidence and unknown line totals stay reviewable rather than silently changing the original estimate.

Within Courses, Achats replaces the Après achats label and starts with purchase cards, source amount/date/store and per-store comparable product subtotals. Opening a purchase shows source lines, quantities and historical line amounts, then dated local competitor references. Existing receipt import, review, original-source retrieval, draft cache and revision guards remain the editing workflow.

Amounts normalized to the same physical quantity are separate from actual buyable whole-pack outlay. For 12 eggs and a 15-egg pack at 6 CAD, comparable cost is 6 × 12/15 = 4.80 CAD; buyable outlay is 6 CAD. Show the actual 15-egg format. Label fractional prorata « Calculé »; changing a format without a fractional calculation does not earn this badge. Repeated receipt lines sharing a reference round packs after grouping. Weighed products remain proportional. No inferred density, dose, product count or unknown quantity.

Only a unique, approved, sourced, dated (at most seven days old and unexpired), available, locally confirmed, compatible-mode reference qualifies. Same-product labels must match literally after whitespace/case normalization; alternatives require an approved substitution, household permission and a match/quality note. Costco membership must be recorded as existing; proposed/unknown required membership is excluded. Ambiguous alternatives, unknown availability, unconfirmed formats/conditional loyalty prices and incompatible currencies/units fail closed. Missing stores remain visible. Partial subtotals show line coverage and never claim a complete basket. No competitive savings claim compares a historical paid total against products excluding taxes/fees.

All competitor amounts here are product costs before taxes, deposits and order fees. Known product tax and deposit details remain visible; unknowns stay unknown. No all-in order total is fabricated. Catalogue/pricing management remains in existing Sources et outils; no new scraper or retailer API is introduced.

Additive optional receipt `sourceType`: unknown, confirmation, final-receipt. Legacy records remain readable with unknown final payment. Only reviewed saved explicit final receipts say paid. Order confirmations never become final receipts through save/delivery alone. New extraction asks for evidence-backed classification; editor permits explicit source review. New metadata writes require `grocery-source-type-v1` on the worker, preventing older workers dropping classification. No worker is installed/restarted by this change. Existing unknown legacy monthly observations are retained for compatibility; explicitly classified confirmations are excluded from monthly spend.

Public-source feasibility checked 2026-10-21: [Costco Same-day help](https://www.costco.ca/f/-/sameday-grocery-help/) separates delivery markups from warehouse prices; [Fresh St. Market delivery/pickup](https://www.freshstmarket.com/grocery-delivery-and-pickup) and [FAQ](https://www.freshstmarket.com/faq) describe online prices and variable pickup service fees. These pages do not establish a priced local basket. Existing reviewed generic-product public research is the supported path; no proprietary API access, checkout, personal-data submission, store contact or cart mutation.
