# FH-GROCERY-012 — Resolve incomplete purchase comparisons

Status: Testing. Related: #155, FH-GROCERY-010/011. Worker 2.28.2 and capability `grocery-purchase-resolution-v1` are required for confirmation writes. Publication, installed activation, private evidence ingestion and phone acceptance are separate gates. FH-GROCERY-013 supersedes the exact-only display restriction by adding a separately labeled estimated scenario; these confirmation and source-preservation rules remain in force.

Achats must explain missing coverage and let the user resolve a line from the comparison: unknown purchase format, candidate identity, absent/expired/nonlocal price and unreviewed equivalence remain distinct. Coverage is computed from eligible rows only. Partial product subtotals and whole-pack outlay remain separate; no historical source total is compared with a competitor subtotal to claim net savings.

`grocery-purchase-resolution.ts` validates immutable dated public candidates bound to the exact source line key, product reference, physical format and optional catalogue article. `grocery-purchase-resolution-store.ts` keeps candidates and explicit, reversible decisions in private `groceries/purchase-resolution.json`, independently of original receipts, prices, cart and notification history. Authenticated GET/POST `/groceries/purchase-resolution` reuses pairing and revision checks. Confirmation requires an explicit affirmative action, rejects changed/contradictory sources and ambiguous confirmed choices, and records when/by which stated source the decision was made. Cancel makes no request. Removing confirmation preserves the candidate and restores unresolved comparison coverage.

Candidate catalogue identity/format and historical purchase confirmation are different facts. Catalogue presence, matching price and similar name never establish a past purchase. A household-supplied mass is labeled an assumption, with its own provenance; it is never recast as an official weight. Confirmation projects a comparison-only line and leaves the raw uncertain source and its payment status unchanged. Source/price freshness, locality, availability, equivalence, units, quantities, membership and format guards still apply. A confirmed format alone cannot create a competitor price.

## Existing and future purchases

The existing 15-minute scanner performs bounded local reconciliation, without retailer requests, new schedules or keys. Only an explicit `productReference: {provider: "pcexpress", id}` in the source may reuse a previously confirmed format. The automatic mail adapter preserves an ID only from one unambiguous first-party public product link in that product's row. Tracking links, names, prices and unrelated cart snapshots cannot supply it. Old evidence keys remain unchanged when the additive reference is absent; changed IDs invalidate bindings. Older clients omitting an ID while editing the same source line retain it.

Automatic reuse requires a unique consistent physical format, a public-source proof no older than seven days and no contradiction with the new source. Quantities come from the new purchase. Household assumptions, stale format proofs and conflicting formats become unconfirmed candidates. Unknown identities stay actionable and unresolved. Reconciliation deduplicates across restarts and does not reconfirm a decision the user removed. Withdrawing or changing the original source invalidates its automatic reuse; a subsequent explicit confirmation of the new purchase remains independent. It never refreshes the dates of old prices or formats. Current price guards continue to exclude expired/nonlocal offers; external price research remains the existing bounded workflow.

An unconfirmed decision for an unchanged source line blocks all later automatic confirmations for that line, including when a newer catalogue proof creates a new candidate. New evidence remains inspectable and can be explicitly confirmed; it cannot override a withdrawal. Implementation references: PR #268 and its focused withdrawal follow-up.
